Real routed workspace windows support desktop dragging/resizing within a viewport-fixed safe band, keyboard move/resize/reset, and transient geometry retained across minimize/maximize. Pointerup applies the final pointer position; cancel/lost capture stop the gesture. Geometry controls are disabled while minimized/maximized, and mobile windows remain full-safe-area views.
